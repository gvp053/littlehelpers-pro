Privacy Policy

Application: Little Helpers
Effective Date: April 18, 2026
Contact: info@littlehelpers.pro

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Little Helpers collects, uses, and protects the personal data of its users. By using the application, you agree to the terms of this policy.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following data:

  • Email address — for registration and login
  • Family member names — for display within the app
  • Tasks, rewards, and points data — for app functionality
  • Device information — to ensure the app works correctly

We do not collect geolocation, payment details, photos, or any other personal information.

3. How We Use Your Data

Collected data is used solely for:

  • User registration and authentication
  • Providing the family space functionality within the app
  • Storing tasks, points, and rewards
  • Improving app quality and user experience

4. Data Storage

Data is stored on Google Firebase servers located in secure data centers. We use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Google Firebase complies with international data security standards including GDPR.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, transfer, or disclose your personal data to third parties, except as required by law.

6. Children's Data

Little Helpers is intended for use by parents. Children's data (names, points, tasks) is entered by parents and stored within the family account. We do not collect data from children directly and do not share it with third parties.

7. Data Security

We take all necessary technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, modification, or deletion. All data is transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the app:

Go to Settings → Delete Account

All your data will be permanently deleted immediately upon confirmation. This action cannot be undone.

9. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy. We will notify users of significant changes through the app or via email.
